FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers to help you get started.

Do you offer beginner-friendly programs?

Yes! All our programs are scalable to fit your current fitness level. Our trainers will guide you step-by-step so you feel comfortable from day one.

What are your membership options?

We offer flexible monthly plans ranging from starter gym access to premium personal training tiers tailored to your goals.

Do I need a personal trainer?

It is not mandatory, but having a personal trainer helps you learn proper form, stay accountable, and reach your goals significantly faster.

What should I bring to the gym?

Bring a water bottle, a small sweat towel, comfortable athletic clothing, and clean indoor training shoes.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes, we have certified one-on-one personal trainers available across all our membership plans or as specialized packages.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday through Sunday from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM to accommodate your busy schedule.

Can I visit before joining?

Absolutely! You can schedule a club tour or drop by during working hours to check out our facilities first-hand.

Do you provide fitness assessments?

Yes, our Popular and Premium membership tiers include structured fitness assessments to measure your progress over time.
